    📖 Engineer's Handbook

    Material properties

    • σ_y — yield strength (MPa). Stress at which permanent deformation begins.
    • ρ — density (g/cm³). Multiply by thickness for per-area weight.
    • ε_f — ductility. High = bends, low = snaps.
    • T_max — upper service temperature.

    Heat treatment

    • Water quench from austenite → martensite (hard, brittle).
    • Air cool → pearlite (balanced).
    • Slow anneal → ferrite + pearlite (soft, ductile).
    • Overheat → grain growth, brittle penalty.

    Failure

    If the delivered part misses spec, you'll get an autopsy with the stress-strain curve and a marker showing where the load fell. Use it.
