HOW TO PLAY

🎮 CONTROLS

Desktop: Hold W A S D to tilt the platform and roll the ball. Press Space to jump.

Mobile: Physically tilt your device — the gyroscope drives the ball.

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Each level teaches a physics concept. Complete the on-screen challenge to unlock a short quiz, then move to the next world.

Sandbox mode lets you tune every parameter freely — no goal, just exploration.

🌍 LEVELS

W1 Corridor — Position vs Time: place 8 snapshot points as you roll.

W2 Path vs Arrow — Distance vs Displacement.

W3 Velocity Aim — Velocity vs Speed: hit the target gate.

W4 Accelerator — Constant acceleration: reach the speed zone.

W5 Drop Tower — Free fall: measure g by rolling off a height.

W6 Create — Design your own drop experiment on any planet.

⚗️ LAB CONTROLS (Sandbox)

μ Friction — how much the surface grips the ball.

m Mass — heavier balls resist acceleration.

gY Gravity — lower it to simulate the Moon or Mars.

Cd Drag — air resistance coefficient.

ρ Air density — affects how much drag the ball feels.
