How to Play
Objective
Match your wave (green) to the hidden target wave (red). Adjust the sliders until they overlap perfectly.
Controls
01 Click/tap and drag the sliders to adjust the amplitude (A), frequency (f), and phase (φ) of your wave.
02 Once multiple waves are unlocked, new sliders appear for each subwave — adjust them independently.
03 Score a point each time you match the target. Difficulty increases with each correct answer.
